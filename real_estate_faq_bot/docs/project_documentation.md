# Real Estate FAQ Bot – Project Documentation

## 1. Introduction

The Real Estate FAQ Bot is designed to assist property buyers by answering frequently asked questions related to pricing, documentation, loans, and buying processes.

---

## 2. System Architecture

The system consists of three main components:

- Frontend: Streamlit-based UI
- Backend Logic: NLP-based similarity engine
- Knowledge Base: Compressed real estate FAQs

---

## 3. NLP Methodology

TF-IDF vectorization is used to convert textual questions into numerical form. Cosine similarity identifies the closest matching question from the dataset.

---

## 4. Data Description

The dataset consists of curated and compressed real estate market knowledge covering:

- Property pricing
- Legal documentation
- Loan availability
- Registration and stamp duty
- Buying considerations

---

## 5. Advantages

- Reduces dependency on human agents
- Provides instant responses
- Easy to scale and maintain

---

## 6. Limitations

- Limited to predefined FAQ data
- No real-time market updates

---

## 7. Conclusion

This project demonstrates how NLP can be effectively applied to real-world industry problems like real estate assistance.
